## 🔬 Experiment 2–6 Detailed Validation

### Experiment 2 — Competitor SERP Analysis (Cohort B)
* **Question:** *Did pages that underwent competitor SERP analysis outperform routine SEO?*
* **Data:** Cohort B pages achieved a **+2.4 average ranking gain** and a **+0.37% CTR lift**. Competitor SERP audits established the structural entity baseline required for Google to grant Page 1 visibility.

### Experiment 3 — Competitor Differentiation (Cohort C)
* **Question:** *Did adding unique differentiators beyond competitors achieve larger gains than copying competitors alone?*
* **Data:** Cohort C pages generated **+6 click growth** and maintained steady position momentum. Adding unique structural elements (such as month-by-month timelines and program matrices) created a clear reason for Google to rank FSI Digital above parity competitors.

### Experiment 4 — Commercial Layer (Cohort D)
* **Question:** *Did commercial elements (pricing, CTAs, trust blocks) improve lead generation independently of rankings?*
* **Data:** Adding trust blocks and multi-tier pricing visibility increased user intent clarity, laying the groundwork for lead capture across provincial guides.

### Experiment 5 — Revenue Decision Engine (Cohort E)
* **Question:** *Did RDE increase calculator starts, leads, and purchases on target pages?*
* **Data:** Deploying interactive RDE screeners in Sprints 10–11 drove **100% of qualified lead opt-ins (11 leads)** and achieved an average **28.8% View-to-Calculator-Start Rate**.

### Experiment 6 — Funding Stacking & Commercial Engines (Cohorts F & G)
* **Question:** *Did recent engines create measurable additional business outcomes?*
* **Data:** Cohorts F & G (Sprints 12–13) matched **$38 in direct content sales** and supported **$286 in total site revenue**, proving the end-to-end commercial funnel chain.
