## 1. Page: `/canada/small-business-grants`
*   **Target Query:** `small business funding canada` (Avg Pos: 61.3 | Impressions: 557)
*   **Top 5 Competitors:**
    1.  *Canada.ca* (Official CSBFP & ISED directory)
    2.  *Innovation Canada* (Interactive Funding Tool)
    3.  *BDC.ca* (SME Financing Hub)
    4.  *Ryan.com* (Tax Advisory / Stacking Guide)
    5.  *Mentor Growth / Enkel* (SME Grant Lists)

### 🔍 Gaps & Intent Profile
*   **Missing Structural Sections:** Competitors feature interactive region/province selectors and direct program filters. Our page lacks a clear "Province/Territory Stacking Quick-Filter Table."
*   **True Freshness Updates:**
    *   **CSBFP 2026 Expansion:** Note that CSBFP now includes working capital loans up to $150,000 (previously limited to physical assets like equipment/property).
    *   **CDAP Sunset:** Mark CDAP (Canada Digital Adoption Program) Boost Your Technology grant as closed/sunset, but mention remaining provincial digital transformation grant alternatives.
*   **Common Mistakes Section:** Target `why grant rejected` / `grant application mistakes`:
    *   *Mistake 1:* Submitting retroactively (most Canadian grants do not fund activities started before signing the contribution agreement).
    *   *Mistake 2:* Ineligible Incorporation Status (unincorporated sole proprietors applying for corporation-only grants).
*   **Contextual Calculator Headline:** *"Check if your business qualifies for Canadian SME funding in under 60 seconds."*
*   **Topic Cluster Link Pathway:** `Government Grants Canada` &rarr; `Small Business Grants Canada` &rarr; `Ontario Small Business Grants` / `Alberta Small Business Grants` &rarr; `Filing Services`.
