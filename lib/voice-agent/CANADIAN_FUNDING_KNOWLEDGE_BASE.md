## 2. CORE CANADIAN FUNDING PROGRAMS CHEAT-SHEET

### A. NRC-IRAP (Industrial Research Assistance Program)
- **Administering Body:** National Research Council of Canada (NRC).
- **Funding Type:** Non-repayable non-dilutive grant (wage subsidy & technical contractor costs).
- **Amount:** Typically $50,000 to $500,000+ (covers 50% to 80% of internal technical salaries, up to 50% of contractor costs).
- **Eligibility:**
  - Canadian incorporated business (CCPC preferred), profit-oriented.
  - 1 to 500 full-time equivalent employees.
  - Dedicated internal technical team solving an engineering or scientific challenge.
  - Must demonstrate financial capability to cover the matching 20% to 50% portion.
- **Intake:** Continuous / Rolling intake. Requires meeting with an Industrial Technology Advisor (ITA).

### B. SR&ED (Scientific Research and Experimental Development)
- **Administering Body:** Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) + Provincial Tax Authorities.
- **Funding Type:** Refundable tax credit (cash back) or non-refundable tax credit.
- **Amount:** Up to 35% federal refundable credit on eligible R&D expenditures (salaries, overhead, materials, 80% of Canadian contractor fees). Provincial top-ups add 10% to 30% (e.g. Ontario OIDTC, Quebec, BC).
- **Eligibility Criteria (The 3 Tests):**
  1. **Technological Uncertainty:** The problem cannot be resolved using standard industry practices.
  2. **Systematic Investigation:** Hypothesis, testing, analysis, documentation.
  3. **Technological Advancement:** Generates new technical knowledge or capability.
- **Filing Deadline:** 18 months after the corporation's fiscal year-end (ideal: file with corporate T2 tax return within 6 months).

### C. CanExport SMEs
- **Administering Body:** Trade Commissioner Service (Global Affairs Canada).
- **Funding Type:** Cost-share grant (50% matching).
- **Amount:** Up to $50,000 per project.
- **Eligible Expenses:** International trade show participation, travel, international market research, legal/regulatory IP certification abroad, foreign digital marketing and SEO.
- **Eligibility:**
  - Canadian incorporated business.
  - $100,000 to $100,000,000 in declared revenue during the previous fiscal year.
  - 1 to 500 employees.

### D. CSBFP (Canada Small Business Financing Program)
- **Administering Body:** Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) delivered via major financial institutions (RBC, TD, BMO, CIBC, Scotiabank, Credit Unions).
- **Funding Type:** Government-guaranteed commercial loan (85% government guarantee).
- **Amount:** Up to $1.15 million total:
  - Up to $1,000,000 for real estate / land.
  - Up to $500,000 for equipment and leasehold improvements.
  - Up to $150,000 for intangible assets and working capital costs.
- **Interest Rate:** Regulated maximum (typically Prime + 3% to 3.75%).

### E. Regional Development Agencies (RDAs)
- **Agencies:** FedDev Ontario, PrairiesCan (AB, SK, MB), PacifiCan (BC), ACOA (Atlantic Canada), CED (Quebec), FedNor (Northern Ontario).
- **Programs:** Business Scale-up and Productivity (BSP), Regional Innovation Ecosystems (RIE).
- **Amount:** $250,000 to $5,000,000 (often 0% interest repayable loans or non-repayable contributions).
- **Focus:** Commercializing advanced technology, automated manufacturing, clean tech, expanding export markets.

### F. Hiring & Wage Subsidies
- **Programs:** BioTalent Canada, ECO Canada, Mitacs Accelerate, Venture for Canada, Magnet Student Work Placement Program (SWPP).
- **Coverage:** 50% to 70% of student or recent graduate wages (up to $5,000 to $10,000 per 4-month placement).

## 3. CAPITAL STACKING RULES & COMMON MISTAKES

### How Stacking Works Legally:
- **Rule 1: Stacking Cap.** Most Canadian government programs have a stacking cap of 75% to 100% of total project costs. You cannot fund 100% of a salary with both IRAP and another grant simultaneously.
- **Rule 2: Combining IRAP & SR&ED.** 
  - IRAP funds salaries upfront (e.g., 80% coverage).
  - The remaining 20% uncovered salary and associated overhead can still be claimed under SR&ED!
  - SR&ED requires subtracting government assistance received from the total pool to avoid "double-dipping."
- **Rule 3: Stacking Federal + Provincial Grants.**
  - A company can receive federal hiring grants and provincial innovation vouchers as long as they fund distinct cost categories or honor the overall stacking limit.
